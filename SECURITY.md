# Security

Report vulnerabilities **privately**. Do not open a public GitHub issue for security problems.

## Contact

Email: [info@simy.ch](mailto:info@simy.ch)  
Subject: `[SECURITY]` plus a short summary

Wir lesen das unter derselben Adresse wie den normalen Support. Schreibe **SECURITY** in den Betreff, dann wird es priorisiert.

## What to include

- Affected host, endpoint, or file
- Who can do what (impact)
- Steps to reproduce
- Optional: how we can reach you

## Scope

**In scope:** `app.simy.ch`, `simy.ch`, this repository, and the production APIs behind them.

**Out of scope:** denial of service, social engineering, physical access, and findings that only work with an already stolen staff/admin session unless you also show how that session is obtained.

## Response

We aim to acknowledge within **3 business days**. For confirmed high or critical issues we aim to ship a fix or a status update within **14 days**.

Please give us a reasonable window before public disclosure.
