### F-01 — Debug calendar sync sends wrong cron auth header *(open since 2026-09-25)*

- **Caller:** `server/api/debug/sync-calendars.post.ts` (outbound `$fetch` to cron)
- **Callee:** `server/api/cron/sync-external-calendars.post.ts` via `assertCronRequest()` (`server/utils/cron-auth.ts`)
- **Issue:** Caller forwards `CRON_SECRET` as `x-api-key`. Callee accepts only `Authorization: Bearer $CRON_SECRET`. Effective missing cron credential on the S2S call → always 401.
