Simy is the all-in-one app for your SMB in Switzerland. Appointments, clients, team and billing — with separate logins for clients, staff and admin. One app for many industries.

BUILT FOR THESE INDUSTRIES
Driving school · Coaching · Consulting · Personal training · Tutoring · Music school · Dog training · Massage
More verticals with industry templates at simy.ch/branchen.

THREE LOGINS IN ONE APP
• Client: book, reschedule and cancel appointments, reminders, progress, payments and invoices
• Staff / team: manage calendar and availability, look after clients, run sessions
• Admin: run the business — team, locations, pricing, billing and reports

AS A CLIENT
• Book, reschedule and cancel appointments online
• Push reminders for upcoming sessions
• Sync appointments to your calendar
• View progress, notes and materials
• Manage payments, credits and invoices
• Sign in quickly with biometrics (fingerprint / face unlock)

AS STAFF / TEAM
• Keep day and week under control
• Manage availability and client contacts
• Document and continue sessions

AS ADMIN
• Manage team and locations
• Control prices, packages and billing
• See reports and business overview

PRIVACY
Your data is processed in Switzerland. We use modern security standards with encryption and biometric authentication.

ACCESS
Your provider (driving school, coach, studio, practice …) sets up your access. Self-signup without an invitation is intentionally disabled. No access yet? Ask your provider about Simy or visit simy.ch.
